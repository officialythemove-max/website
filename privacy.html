Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information The Move ("The Move," "we," "us") collects through The Move app and this website (the "Service"), how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have. It's part of our Terms and Conditions.

The short version. We collect what we need to run the app: your name, birthday, email, photo, any socials you add and the bars you pick. Other users see your name, photo, socials and plans, never your email or birthday. We don't sell your information, show ads or track you across other apps. New profile photos are checked automatically by OpenAI's moderation service. You can delete your account and data in the app at any time.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, date of birth, email address and password. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so we can't see it.
  • Profile photo: the photo of yourself that you add.
  • Socials (optional): any Instagram or Snapchat usernames you add.
  • Plans: the bar you pick for a night, or that you're staying in.
  • Settings and agreements: whether "Hide my face and socials" is on, and which version of our Terms you agreed to and when.
  • Safety activity: people you block, reports you send (who and why), and reports other users send about you.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to our support email.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical information: when the app talks to our servers, our service providers log technical details such as your IP address, device and app version, and the time of the request. We use these logs to keep the Service running and secure.

What we don't collect

  • The Move doesn't access your device's location, contacts, camera or microphone. It opens your photo library only when you choose a photo, and only the photo you pick is uploaded.
  • If you turn on notifications, the reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. We don't collect a push token or any other information to send them, and they don't come from our servers.
  • We don't collect payment information. The Move is free.
  • We don't use advertising, ad identifiers, third-party analytics or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. This website doesn't use cookies.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, confirm your email address, keep your account secure and let you reset your password.
  • To check that you're 18 or older.
  • To show your profile and plans to other users, and to show how many people are going to each bar.
  • To check new profile photos for nudity, violence and other content that isn't allowed (see section 4).
  • To review reports, enforce our Terms, prevent abuse and keep users safe.
  • To send account emails, such as verification codes and important notices about your account or our Terms. We don't send marketing emails unless you separately agree to them.
  • To fix problems, improve the Service and comply with the law.

3. What other users can see

Other signed-in users can see your name, profile photo, any Instagram or Snapchat usernames you add, and the bar you pick for a night. They can't see your email address, date of birth or password.

If you turn on "Hide my face and socials," you still count toward a bar's total, but other users see a blank avatar and no socials. Profile photos are stored at a web address that the app uses to display them, so anyone who has that address can view the photo.

4. Who helps us run The Move

We use a few service providers. They handle your information only to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in system and photo storage on servers in the United States.
  • Resend sends our account emails, such as verification and password reset codes. It receives your email address and the content of those emails.
  • OpenAI checks each new profile photo for content that isn't allowed. It receives the photo only, not your name or email. OpenAI says it doesn't use this data to train its models and keeps it no longer than 30 days unless the law requires otherwise. Photos it flags are deleted from our storage right away and are never shown to other users.
  • Apple distributes the app through the App Store. Apple's own privacy policy covers what it collects.

When you tap a link to Instagram, Snapchat, a ticket seller or Apple Maps, you leave The Move, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

5. When else we share information

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. Apart from the service providers above, we share information only:

  • when the law requires it, such as in response to a valid court order;
  • when we believe in good faith that it's needed to protect someone's safety, or to investigate fraud or a serious breach of our Terms;
  • as part of a merger, sale or reorganization of The Move, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

6. How long we keep it

  • We keep your information while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account (Profile, then "Delete my account"), we delete your account, profile, photo, plans, blocks and any reports about you. Reports you sent about other users may be kept without your name attached, so we can keep handling safety issues.
  • Copies in our service providers' logs and backups may remain for a limited time before they're removed on those providers' normal schedules.
  • We keep support emails as long as we need them to help you, and longer if the law requires.

7. Your choices

  • You can change your photo and socials, turn on "Hide my face and socials," change your password and delete your account at any time in the app.
  • Notifications are off unless you turn them on. You can turn them off at any time with the Notifications switch in your profile or in your phone's settings.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@officiallythemove.com from the address on your account. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have other privacy rights under your state's law. We'll honor the requests above wherever you live.

8. Age limit

The Move is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we'll delete it. If you think a person under 18 is using The Move, please email us.

9. Security

Information is encrypted in transit, passwords are hashed, and database rules limit each user to the information they're allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email support@officiallythemove.com.

You must be 21 or older to drink. The Move doesn't support underage drinking.
