Help and support

Contact us: support@officiallythemove.com

Email us about anything: account problems, wrong bar info, privacy requests or safety concerns. We review reports about users and photos within 24 hours.

In an emergency, call 911. The Move can't send help.

Safety

Report or block someone

Open the bar they're going to, tap their face in the list of who's going, then tap Report or Block. Blocking hides them from you right away. Reports come to us, and we can remove photos or close accounts that break our rules.

Report a photo or account by email

Email us the person's name, the bar and night where you saw them, and what happened.

Signing in

I didn't get my code

  • Check your spam, junk and promotions folders for an email from The Move.
  • Codes expire after 15 minutes. Tap Send a new code to get a fresh one.
  • Make sure the email address is spelled right.

I forgot my password

On the log-in screen, tap Forgot password?. We'll email you a code, and then you can choose a new password.

Your profile

Why was my photo turned down?

New photos are checked automatically. Photos with nudity, violence or other content that isn't allowed are blocked. Use a clear photo of yourself.

I don't want people to see me

Open your profile and turn on Hide my face and socials. You still count toward a bar's total, but you show up as a blank avatar with no socials.

Deleting your account

Open your profile, tap Delete my account, then tap it again to confirm. This removes your account, profile, photo and plans right away.

Can't get into the app? Email support@officiallythemove.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. See our Privacy Policy for what we delete and keep.

Who can use The Move

You must be 18 or older to use The Move, and 21 or older to drink. Each bar sets and checks its own age rules, and many are 21 and over.

You must be 21 or older to drink. The Move doesn't support underage drinking.
